效度與使用限制

  • 內容對應：題目依 SIMS 四構面編碼分組，可支持量表內容與理論架構的對應說明。
  • 建構效度限制：兩個時點的完整樣本數都低於 16 題的五倍門檻（80），不將 KMO、Bartlett、EFA 或 CFA 視為穩定的建構效度證據。
  • 比較建議：若要檢驗前後差異，應以 35 筆已配對的識別碼建立配對資料，再預先決定分析方式與報告效果量。

Summary and sample

Both time points are complete. After reverse scoring, overall internal consistency is good: Cronbach’s α = .927 at pretest and .893 at posttest. All four SIMS subscales meet at least an acceptable reliability level for describing motivation at each survey time point.

Interpretation boundary: The two files contain 39 and 41 responses respectively, with only 35 matched identifiers. Cross-time results here are descriptive only; no paired statistical test was run, so this report does not claim a teaching effect.

Data quality and scoring

Good completeness: Both workbooks have 16 response-item columns. There are no missing item responses among valid records, and every item has response variation.
  • Score direction: Likert direction was read from the leading 1–7 score in the full-text response-record worksheet.
  • Construct mapping: Intrinsic motivation (Q1, Q5, Q9, Q13); identified regulation (Q2, Q6, Q10, Q14); external regulation (Q3, Q7, Q11, Q15); amotivation (Q4, Q8, Q12, Q16).
  • Overall reliability: Q4, Q8, Q12, and Q16 were reverse-scored only for the 16-item overall alpha. Amotivation means retain their original direction: a higher score means higher amotivation.

Validity and limits

  • Content alignment: Grouping follows the four SIMS constructs, supporting a content-to-theory alignment statement.
  • Construct-validity limit: Each complete sample is below five times the 16-item count (80). KMO, Bartlett, EFA, and CFA are not reported as stable construct-validity evidence.
  • Comparison recommendation: Use the 35 matched identifiers to create a paired dataset, then pre-specify the statistical test and effect-size reporting before testing pre/post change.
  • Criterion validity: No external criterion or additional instrument was supplied, so this evidence was not tested.
